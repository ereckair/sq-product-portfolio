INTRO
BUSINESS CONTEXT

External Commodity Data Is Not Linked to Internal Material Categories

Without reliable Commodity-to-Material Category mapping, analysis across Costing BOMs, Supplier quotations and Vendor quotations remains manual.
01 · AVAILABLE DATA

External market data is available

Commodity prices, regional market data and historical trends are available.
02 · CONNECTION GAP

The Category link is missing

Commodity signals are not consistently mapped to internal Material Categories.
  • Commodity and Material Category are not connected
  • Names and specifications across data sources require manual matching
  • Weights, alerts and calculation rules are fragmented
03 · BUSINESS IMPACT

Business analysis remains manual

Teams manually map the data before analyzing cost, quotations and negotiation opportunities.
  • The impact of market changes must be recalculated manually
  • Quotation decisions lack a consistent market reference
  • Price reduction opportunities and negotiation materials are prepared ad hoc
Objective: create a reliable connection from market signals to costing, quotations and negotiations.
